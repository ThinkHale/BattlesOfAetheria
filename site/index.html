AETHERIA RISING

Clash of the Crossing

Thirteen commanders of Rome, Egypt, Persia and Han, drawn out of their own centuries by the mist. Fight through them all. Then fight yourself.

The game

  • 13 commanders from Aetheria Rising, each with their own signature Special, a devastating Crossing Art, their own lines, a rival, and an arcade epilogue in their own voice.
  • Arcade, Versus, Two Players and Training. Arcade ends against the Echo of the Crossing: the mist, wearing your face.
  • A real fighting system: chains, cancels, throws and breaks, armour, counters, juggles and supers, at a steady 60 frames a second.
  • Four CPU difficulties, from Squire to Legend.
  • Five battlefields: the Forum, the Nile, the Gate of All Nations, the Juyan watchtower and the Crossing.
  • Touch controls you can resize and move, plus game controllers and keyboards.
  • No ads, no purchases, no account, no data collected.
